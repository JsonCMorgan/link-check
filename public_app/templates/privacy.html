What we do with your input

By default, your URL, message text, or QR image is processed on the server in memory. We do not open the suspicious link.

If you tick Check official careers page, we HTTP-fetch only URLs listed in our employer config (for example careers.intactfc.com for Intact). We never fetch the link you pasted. We scan that HTML for hiring-vendor names. Your hosting provider may log those outbound requests.

We do not send your paste to VirusTotal, Google Safe Browsing, or similar.

QR photos are decoded in memory and discarded. They are not written to disk as part of the check.

Logs

We do not persist full URLs or messages in a database in v0.1. Hosting providers may log HTTP requests (IP, path) in standard web logs. Check your host’s policy if you self-deploy.

Rate limits

Public instance applies a simple per-IP rate limit to reduce abuse.

Not legal advice

Output is educational heuristics for anyone checking suspicious links (texts, email, QR, social). When in doubt, open the organization’s official site yourself or call a number you already trust.
