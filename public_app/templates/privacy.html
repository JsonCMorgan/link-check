What we do with your paste

In version 0.1, your URL is parsed on the server in memory to extract the hostname and compare it to the company name you optionally enter. We do not HTTP-fetch the link. We do not send the URL to VirusTotal, Google Safe Browsing, or similar services unless a future version adds an explicit opt-in button (none today).

Logs

We do not persist full URLs in a database in v0.1. Hosting providers may log HTTP requests (IP, path) in standard web logs. Check your host’s policy if you self-deploy.

Rate limits

Public instance applies a simple per-IP rate limit to reduce abuse.

Not legal advice

Output is educational heuristics for job seekers and analysts. When in doubt, open the company’s official website yourself.
